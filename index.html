Our Heritage

About Rose Garden

Founded in 1998, Rose Garden began as a passion project to preserve vintage heirloom English roses and create romantic botanical spaces.

Our Story

What started as a small 2-acre rose patch has grown into a premier 25-acre botanical estate. We cultivate over 120 historic and modern rose varieties, supplying garden lovers, boutique hotels, and grand event organizers across the country.

Our commitment remains simple: to deliver unmatched vigor, captivating perfumes, and sustainable garden practices in every plant and arrangement we craft.

Our Mission

To inspire and enrich lives through the beauty of artisanal roses, providing exceptional plant quality, sustainable organic practices, and personalized landscape design services.

Our Vision

To be the world's most trusted destination for rose lovers, championing heirloom plant conservation, horticultural education, and breathtaking floral artistic design.
